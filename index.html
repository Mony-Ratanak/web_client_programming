Key Concept — Read This First

Domain Name vs Web Hosting — What's the Difference?

These are two completely separate purchases from two different types of companies. You need both for a website to work.

🏡

Web Hosting

The physical land and house where your files live

  • A server (computer) running 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Stores your PHP files, images, database, themes, plugins
  • You pay a monthly or yearly fee to rent server space
  • Without hosting: your site's files have no home on the internet
Examples: Hostinger, SiteGround, InfinityFree (free), DigitalOcean
+ = working website
📮

Domain Name

The street address visitors type in their browser

  • The human-readable address: e.g. soktechnews.com
  • Points visitors' browsers to your hosting server's IP address
  • You lease it (1–10 years) — you never permanently own it
  • Without a domain: visitors can't find your site (they'd need the IP address)
Examples: Namecheap, GoDaddy, Google Domains

Top-Level Domains (TLDs) — The Extension After the Dot

The extension at the end of a domain name determines its purpose, audience, and requirements.

💡 Important: You LEASE a domain — you never own it permanently Nobody "buys" a domain forever. You register it for 1–10 years. If you don't renew it on time, anyone else can register it. ICANN (the Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers) is the global organisation that governs all domain registrations worldwide.

Types of Web Hosting

Not all hosting is the same. The right choice depends on your site's traffic, your budget, and how much technical control you need.

SSL Certificates — The Padlock Icon (🔒)

SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encrypts the connection between a visitor's browser and your server. It's what turns http:// into https:// and shows the padlock icon.

✅
HTTPS site: Data is encrypted. Passwords and form submissions are protected. Google ranks you higher in search results.
❌
HTTP site: Chrome shows "Not Secure" warning. Visitors see a red warning. Google penalises your ranking. Login forms are a security risk.
🔒 https://yoursite.com
Connection is secure
⚠ Not Secure http://oldsite.com
Your connection is not private

💡 Most hosting providers include Free SSL via Let's Encrypt — always enable it!

⚠ The Renewal Trap — The Most Important Lesson Today

This is a real-world lesson that could save you (or your future clients) hundreds of dollars.

How to Avoid the Renewal Trap:

  • ✅ Always search "[provider name] renewal price" before buying
  • ✅ Look for "renews at" text next to the promotional price
  • ✅ Set a calendar reminder 30 days before your subscription expires to shop around
  • ✅ Consider buying 2–3 years upfront at the promotional rate before it jumps
  • ✅ Compare multiple providers using your hosting comparison table
